=========================================
    GACHA STUDY INFO
=========================================

=========================================
Inspiration
=========================================

I have been playing alot of virtual cottage and hades 2 recently, and I thought it would be 
interesting to combine Hades 2's keepsake system with a study timer to help motivate me and 
other people into studying. Even though the study timers(eg: virtual cottage) I use right 
now are useful, I thought I can augment it by combining it with a lootbox system, the 
ultimate motivator. oh yea also + brainrot cuz i thought it would be funny and match the theme

=========================================
What it does
=========================================

Basically, this is a simple study timer, except you get coins when you study passively, 
in which you can use the coins to open lootboxes, which can give you either a brainrot 
keepsake or a consumable of different rarities. The keepsakes acts similar to hades 2, 
it augments your study sessions in different ways like one of them would give you a 
consumable after a certain number of hours of studying. 

=========================================
    GACHA SYSTEM GUIDE      
=========================================

-----------------------------------------
1. BASE ODDS (STANDARD)
-----------------------------------------
These odds apply when no luck-modifying consumables are active.

Rarity      | Threshold  | Probability
------------|------------|------------
Common      | 0.0 - 60.0 | 60.0%
Rare        | 60.1 - 90.0| 30.0%
Epic        | 90.1 - 98.0| 8.0%
Legendary   | 98.1 - 99.9| 1.9%
Mythic      | 99.9 - 100 | 0.1%

-----------------------------------------
2. LUCKY ODDS (MODIFIED RATE BUFF)
-----------------------------------------
Active when "Modified Loot Box Rate" is used. Common and Rare 
rates are reduced to favor higher tiers.

Rarity      | Threshold  | Probability
------------|------------|------------
Common      | 0.0 - 40.0 | 40.0%
Rare        | 40.1 - 75.0| 35.0%
Epic        | 75.1 - 92.0| 17.0%
Legendary   | 92.1 - 99.5| 7.5%
Mythic      | 99.5 - 100 | 0.5% 

-----------------------------------------
3. THE PITY SYSTEM (HARD PITY @ 25)
-----------------------------------------
If you do not pull an Epic or higher for 24 consecutive rolls, 
the 25th roll triggers "Hard Pity."

MECHANIC:
The random roll is forced into a range of 90.0 - 100.0. 
Common and Rare items are removed from the pool entirely.

PITY ODDS (STANDARD):
- Epic:      80.0%
- Legendary: 19.0%
- Mythic:     1.0%

PITY ODDS (LUCKY BUFF ACTIVE):
- Epic:      20.0%
- Legendary: 75.0%
- Mythic:     5.0%

*Note: Pulling an Epic, Legendary, or Mythic naturally 
before roll 50 will reset the Pity Counter to 0

-----------------------------------------
4. ITEM DROP TYPES
-----------------------------------------
When a box is opened, the game determines the "Drop Type":

- 20% Chance: KEEPSAKE 
- 80% Chance: CONSUMABLE 

EXTRA STUFF:
- Duplicates: If you pull a Keepsake you already own at Lv. 5, 
  you receive a Coin Refund based on rarity:
  * Common: 50 | Rare: 100 | Epic: 300 | Legendary: 1000
